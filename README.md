Digital Marketing Performance Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

An end-to-end commercial analytics project evaluating multi-platform digital marketing campaigns across key performance indicators (**CTR, Conversion Rate, ROAS, and Cost Per Conversion**) to identify high-performing channels and budget optimisation opportunities.

---

## Project Objectives
* **Data Hygiene & Standardisation:** Clean, validate, and structure raw multi-channel campaign exports to eliminate duplicates, formatting inconsistencies, and null values.
* **KPI Engineering:** Calculate core marketing efficiency metrics—including Click-Through Rate (CTR), Conversion Rate (CVR), Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
* **Executive Visualisation:** Build a dynamic, interactive Excel dashboard featuring pivot tables, slicers, and trend charts to support rapid stakeholder decision-making.

---

## Analytical Workflow & Tools
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Power Query / Data Cleaning, Dynamic Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas, Interactive Slicers & Charts).
* **Step-by-Step Process:**
  1. **Raw Data Audit:** Inspected campaign logs across platforms, standardising date formats, currency fields, and campaign naming conventions.
  2. **Metric Calculation:** Engineered calculated fields for CTR, CVR, and ROAS across distinct campaign periods and audience segments.
  3. **Dashboard Design:** Constructed an interactive visual summary allowing users to filter performance dynamically by platform, campaign theme, and timeframe.

---

## Key Business Insights
* **Peak Campaign ROI:** The **Winter Sale** campaign consistently drove the highest overall engagement, conversion efficiency, and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) compared to baseline awareness campaigns.
* **Budget Reallocation:** Identified underperforming ad sets with high cost-per-click and low conversion rates, highlighting clear opportunities to shift spend toward high-converting seasonal promotions.
